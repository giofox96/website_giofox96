The project, set in the Tamburi district of Taranto, aims to revitalise the urban fabric. It fills the large urban voids between public housing blocks and introduces a transport hub that overcomes the drop separating the city from its waterfront.

The first phase assessed the area's existing assets: the seascape, the church, a busy shopping mall and a well-placed station. The design builds on them with a colonnade that shapes new squares and connected walkways, tying the neighbourhood's landmarks together.
