The Sh-Rooms pavilion is a sustainable structure made entirely of reused concrete blocks. The design is based on modularity, creating a flexible space that can be configured for different purposes.

The roof is the centrepiece of the design. Its dynamic, sculptural form contrasts with the rigid appearance of the concrete blocks. The roof is self-supporting: a system of interlocking blocks produced with a parametric design algorithm.

Inside, the pavilion is open and airy, with natural light filtering in through the holes. Graffiti on the walls adds a vibrant, colourful touch to the space.
