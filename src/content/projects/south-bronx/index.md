The project is set in the Mott Haven and Melrose neighbourhoods of the Bronx, in New York City, and renovates a section of the coastal area. The site is well connected by public transport and overlooks the water.

The proposal includes the renovation of a storage facility, a new market hall and a large mixed-use building that houses both residences and public services. It connects to the train station and builds a terraced waterfront that addresses flooding.

To reduce the environmental impact of construction, almost every structure is built in laminated timber: glulam pillars and beams, CLT walls and DLT slabs.
