The project proposes a sustainable reimagining of the green area around the Magna PT S.p.A. headquarters. It is a sequence of interconnected plazas linked by a continuous path that makes both existing buildings and new spaces fully accessible.

The outdoor space holds exhibition areas, refreshment zones, new office extensions, an open-air gym and a small amphitheatre. Materials favour natural stone and low-water planting, following xeriscaping principles. The planting combines native southern Italian species, such as olive trees, with others adapted to warm climates.

The project received an honourable mention in the Magna PT competition for the garden of the Modugno site, in May 2022.
