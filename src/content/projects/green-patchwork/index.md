The project is located on the southern coast of Bari and takes the orthogonal grid of the Murattiano district as its reference. The historical grid is reinterpreted to organise a system of green spaces with multiple functions: open-air theatres, urban vegetable gardens, public parks and sports areas.

The design also integrates phytoremediation tanks and artificial sea pools, protecting and enhancing the local coastal ecosystem. All plant species, whether for landscaping or cultivation, are native to the region and selected for their adaptability to the local climate.

The project was presented at the Bari International Archifestival, within the "Mediterranean Parks" initiative for the redevelopment of Bari's southern waterfront.
