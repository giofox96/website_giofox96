Casina delle Mura stands in Palo del Colle, near Bari, on the edge between the town and the olive-growing countryside. The project started from a full survey of the interior and exterior, combining photogrammetry with direct measurement.

The walls were read type by type with the Borri cards, which traced the building's growth through six construction phases, from 1816 to 1960. The assessment mapped the decay of masonry and plaster, the crack patterns and the way the walls are joined, and from these the likely collapse mechanisms.

The restoration project sets out the interventions: steel ties and ring beams, scuci-cuci repair of the cracked masonry, waterproofing at the base of the walls, a new timber roof, consolidation of the vaults and the reintegration of missing parts.
