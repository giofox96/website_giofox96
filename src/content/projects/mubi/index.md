The MuBi is set within the disused "Gran Marrone" marble quarry, in a section of the Ofanto River Valley Park, and blends into its natural surroundings.

The train station sits on the northern side of the area, while broad terraces shaped to follow the natural topography face south, opening onto panoramic views.

The architectural form draws on the crystal structure of pyrite. A space truss carries the building, with beams that follow the edges of icosahedra.
